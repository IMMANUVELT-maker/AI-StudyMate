Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about AI StudyMate

What is AI StudyMate?
+
AI StudyMate is an AI-powered personalized tutoring and adaptive learning platform that uses your study materials to provide grounded tutoring, assessments, feedback, and progress tracking. It learns from your performance and adapts the learning experience around your unique needs.
What study materials can I upload?
+
You can upload a variety of materials including PDF textbooks, lecture notes, presentation slides, lecture videos, research papers, and any other learning materials. The platform supports multiple formats to ensure you can use all your existing study resources.
How does source citation work?
+
AI StudyMate connects every answer to relevant sources from your uploaded materials. It can reference exact textbook pages, presentation slides, lecture video timestamps, and sections of your notes. This ensures you know exactly where information comes from.
What happens if the answer isn't in my material?
+
If a question can't be answered from your uploaded materials, AI StudyMate identifies it as an unsupported question rather than providing information without proper sources. This ensures you only get answers grounded in your actual study material.
How are adaptive quizzes generated?
+
Questions are generated based on multiple factors: the topics you're studying, your current difficulty level, the question type you need (MCQ, short answer, numerical), and signals from your previous performance. This ensures quizzes match your learning level exactly.
Can AI StudyMate identify weak topics?
+
Yes! The system analyzes your assessment performance, quiz results, and answer patterns to identify topics where you consistently struggle or make mistakes. It then recommends targeted practice in these areas to help you improve.
Can I use materials from different subjects together?
+
Yes! You can upload materials from different subjects and the AI tutor will work across all of them. This is particularly useful for integrated courses or when you're studying multiple subjects simultaneously.
